# Underground Caverns 2: The Revenge

A Sprout microworld: Zork II: The Wizard of Frobozz, ported a quarter at a
time, and whole since round 7. The first quarter runs from the barrow to
the Carousel (the way in, the garden and its unicorn, the Carousel Room,
the Riddle Room, the well and its bucket, the Tea Room and its cakes, the
Posts Room and the Pool Room, the robot's rooms); the second is the dragon
and the Bank (the ravine's ledge, the Tiny Room and the Dreary Room, the
Dragon Room and the dragon's lair, the Fresco Room, the Stone Bridge, the
Cool Room and the Ice Room, and the Bank of Zork from its entrance to its
vault); the third the volcano and the Menhir (the Lava Room and the Lava
Tube, the Volcano View, the Cobwebby Corridor, the Volcano Bottom and the
balloon that rises from it through the volcano's air to the Narrow Ledge
and the Library and the Wide Ledge and the Dusty Room with its safe, the
Menhir Room and the Kennel behind the menhir, the Stairway and the nine
Oddly-angled Rooms); and the fourth the Wizard's own rooms and the end
(the Guarded Room and its lizard, the Workshop, the Workroom with the
three stands, the Trophy Room with the cabinet he keeps what he takes in,
his Quarters, the Pentagram Room and the demon it calls, the Aquarium Room
and the Murky Room; the four Rooms of Mist, where the dead go; the
Cerberus Room, the Crypt Anteroom, the Crypt of the Flatheads and the
Landing beyond it). The Wizard of Frobozz walks all of it, the dragon
follows, the princess goes home, the Volcano Gnome shows the stranded the
way out for a fee, and the demon does one thing for ten treasures.

`sprout check .` checks it, and `sprout test .` runs its tests, the
scripts in `tests/`; `tests/walkthrough.json` plays from the barrow to the
Landing, 400 of 400 points, and the others each one puzzle or one round's
answers: the bucket, the cakes, the robot and the cage, the gnome and the
Bank's walls, the riddle, the balloon left untied, the Volcano Gnome, and
each refining round's answers (`revision3` to `revision9`, the seventh and
eighth in parts).

# Underground Caverns 2: The Revenge

A Sprout microworld: Zork II: The Wizard of Frobozz, ported a quarter at a
time. This is the first quarter, from the barrow to the Carousel: the way
in, the garden and its unicorn, the Carousel Room, the Riddle Room, the
well and its bucket, the Tea Room and its cakes, the Posts Room and the
Pool Room, the robot's rooms, and the Wizard of Frobozz himself.

`sprout check .` checks it, and `sprout test .` runs its tests, the
scripts in `tests/`; `tests/walkthrough.json` plays from the barrow to the
end of the quarter.

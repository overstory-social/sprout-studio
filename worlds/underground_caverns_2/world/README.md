# Underground Caverns 2: The Revenge

A Sprout microworld: Zork II: The Wizard of Frobozz, ported a quarter at a
time. Three quarters stand: the first, from the barrow to the Carousel (the
way in, the garden and its unicorn, the Carousel Room, the Riddle Room,
the well and its bucket, the Tea Room and its cakes, the Posts Room and
the Pool Room, the robot's rooms); the second, the dragon and the Bank
(the ravine's ledge, the Tiny Room and the Dreary Room, the Dragon Room
and the dragon's lair, the Fresco Room, the Stone Bridge, the Cool Room
and the Ice Room, and the Bank of Zork from its entrance to its vault);
and the third, the volcano and the Menhir (the Lava Room and the Lava
Tube, the Volcano View, the Cobwebby Corridor, the Volcano Bottom and the
balloon that rises from it through the volcano's air to the Narrow Ledge
and the Library and the Wide Ledge and the Dusty Room with its safe, the
Menhir Room and the Kennel behind the menhir, the Stairway and the nine
Oddly-angled Rooms), with the Wizard of Frobozz walking all of it, the
dragon that follows, the princess that goes home, and the Volcano Gnome
who shows the stranded the way out for a fee.

`sprout check .` checks it, and `sprout test .` runs its tests, the
scripts in `tests/`; `tests/walkthrough.json` plays from the barrow to the
end of the third quarter, 260 of 400 points, and the others each one
puzzle or one round's answers: the bucket, the cakes, the robot and the
cage, the gnome and the Bank's walls, the riddle, the balloon left untied
and the Volcano Gnome.

# Underground Caverns 2: The Revenge

A Sprout microworld: Zork II: The Wizard of Frobozz, ported a quarter at a
time. Two quarters stand: the first, from the barrow to the Carousel (the
way in, the garden and its unicorn, the Carousel Room, the Riddle Room,
the well and its bucket, the Tea Room and its cakes, the Posts Room and
the Pool Room, the robot's rooms), and the second, the dragon and the Bank
(the ravine's ledge, the Tiny Room and the Dreary Room, the Dragon Room
and the dragon's lair, the Fresco Room, the Stone Bridge, the Cool Room
and the Ice Room, and the Bank of Zork from its entrance to its vault),
with the Wizard of Frobozz walking all of it, the dragon that follows and
the princess that goes home.

`sprout check .` checks it, and `sprout test .` runs its tests, the
scripts in `tests/`; `tests/walkthrough.json` plays from the barrow to the
end of the second quarter, 190 of 400 points, and `tests/revision4.json`
the garden's answers about the unicorn's ribbon and key.
